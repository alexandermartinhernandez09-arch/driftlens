## Full description

```
DriftLens checks AI image series in seconds — entirely on your device.

Got a series from Midjourney, DALL·E, Flux or another tool? DriftLens shows which images match your anchor and which drift. Nothing is uploaded. Your files never leave your phone or PC.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Load a series (2–50 images, folder, drag & drop, or video)
2. Set an anchor — your reference image
3. PASS · WARN · FAIL per image and for the whole series
4. Blink mode: verify differences with your eyes

FOR
• Logo & brand series (type, center, color)
• Portraits & people (subject drift, optional face)
• Product mockups (size, format, color)
• Comics, animals & characters (structure, dHash)

FEATURES
• 11+ QA gates: pixels, 9 zones, histogram, dHash, optional face gate
• Video → still frames (frame extraction)
• Blink, slider, heatmap, zones & region view
• Series profiles: logo, portrait, product — auto-detected
• Export QA report (JSON/HTML)
• German & English
• Works offline (face gate needs internet once for model)

HONEST
DriftLens compares pixels and structure — no magic claims. Face gate is optional for portraits; it does not replace your eyes.

Privacy: https://[YOUR-DOMAIN]/privacy.html

Contact: “Report issue” in the app
```
